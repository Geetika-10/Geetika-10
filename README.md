## About Me
I am Geetika, a PhD scholar at the Distributed Computing Learning Lab (DCLL) at Indraprastha Institute of Information Technology (IIIT-D). My research focuses on Mathematical Optimization in Machine Learning. I particularly work in Federated Learning.

## Research Interests
Federated Learning Optimization, Convergence of FL algorithms, Understanding why ML/FL work the way they do.

Wish to work on the theoretical understanding of LLMs in ML/FL.

## Let's Connect
If you are interested in discussing the theoretical aspects of Machine Learning, I would love to discuss and learn from your work and insights.

## Personal Interests

I believe animals suffer the most in our world, and they deserve every effort we can make to improve their lives in any way- feed them, foster them, sponsor their treatment, contribute to a local animal welfare NGO, or any NGO working for your favourite animals like dogs, cats, cows, etc.
